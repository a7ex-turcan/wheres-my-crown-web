A PRINCESS MAZE ADVENTURE

WHERE'S MY CROWN?

The princess has lost her crown somewhere in the maze. Help her find it, tiptoe past the fire-puffing dragon, and make friends with a unicorn, a fairy and a wise old owl along the way.

A gentle 16-bit maze game for little adventurers, about 3 to 7.

A game idea by Nati, age 4

Nati came up with the princess, the maze and the dragon who puffs fire. Her dad built the game around her ideas, one by one: the unicorn that leaves a trail of hearts, the crown the princess has to find, and the little sister who follows her everywhere.

Take a peek

Made for little hands

Big, easy controls

Tap where to go, swipe, or use the big arrow buttons. No fiddly timing needed.

Reads out loud

Every message is spoken, so children who can't read yet can play on their own.

Gentle, never scary

If the dragon puffs fire, the princess simply hops back to a safe spot and tries again.

Dress up

Choose her dress and hair colour, and bring a little sister who follows her around.

Bring a helper

Each helper has its own way of dealing with the dragon, and its own sparkly trail.

Unicorn

Gallops to the dragon and chases it away in a puff of smoke, leaving hearts or a rainbow behind. Then the princess can climb on and ride.

Fairy

Flies over the walls and sprinkles the dragon to sleep, then flutters along beside the princess in a cloud of fairy dust.

Owl

Flies straight to the crown and lights up the way there with a trail of marching purple arrows.

More helpers are on the way: a bunny, a kitten and a turtle.
