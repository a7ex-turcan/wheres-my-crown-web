What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app saves a few things on the device itself. They are never sent to us:

  • which mazes are unlocked;
  • settings such as sound and read-aloud on or off;
  • dress-up choices (dress and hair colours, and whether the little sister comes along);
  • whether the Royal Pass has been bought.

Deleting the app deletes this information.

Purchases

The Royal Pass is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock the helpers. Purchases sit behind a grown-up question so children can't buy by accident.
