Common questions

How do you play?+

Help the princess walk through the maze to her crown. Tap any spot on the path and she walks there by herself, or use the big arrow buttons, or swipe. Watch out for the dragon: if it catches her, she loses a heart and hops back to a safe spot. Finding the crown unlocks the next maze.

What are helpers?+

Before each maze you can bring a helper. The unicorn chases the dragon away (and can be ridden), the fairy puts it to sleep, and the owl shows the way to the crown. In the free version a helper costs one short ad; with the Royal Pass every helper is free.

Why is there a maths question?+

That's the grown-up gate. Purchases and settings are behind a quick multiplication question so little ones can't buy anything or change settings by accident.

I bought the Royal Pass. How do I restore it on a new device?+

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Then on the title screen tap For grown-ups, answer the question, open Royal Pass and tap Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?+

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong with your purchase, email us as well and we'll help.

How do I turn off the sound or the reading voice?+

On the title screen tap For grown-ups, answer the question, then switch Sound or Read aloud off.

How do I start the mazes over?+

Tap For grown-ups on the title screen, answer the question and choose Reset maze progress. All mazes except the first one lock again. Your Royal Pass and dress-up choices are kept.

It's a bit too hard for my child. Any tips?+

Try bringing the fairy or the unicorn: both deal with the dragon so the maze becomes a calm walk. The owl is great for children who get lost, because it draws arrows all the way to the crown.
